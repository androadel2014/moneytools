## Full description
MoneyTools is a fast, offline money calculator for everyday life. No account, no sign-in, no analytics.

Four tools in one app:
- Loan Calculator: see your monthly payment, total interest and total cost before you borrow.
- Tip & Split: work out the tip, the total, and what each person pays.
- Discount & Tax: discount first, then tax on the discounted price — what you pay, what you save, and the tax.
- Savings Growth: see how a starting amount and a monthly deposit grow with monthly compounding.

Simple, plain-English labels, big readable results, and sensible defaults so the first screen is never empty. Everything works offline, forever.

Ads are honest: one small banner at the bottom of Home and below results, and at most one full-screen ad in 3 minutes after a calculation — never while you type, never after your first calculation. One-tap Remove Ads removes every ad forever, with Restore Purchases included.

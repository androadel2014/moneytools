# Privacy Policy — MoneyTools: Loan & Tip Calculator

Last updated: 2026-11-18.

MoneyTools works fully offline. We do not ask you to create an account or sign in, and we do not collect analytics.

## What the app stores on your device
Only three things, in SharedPreferences on your device:
- Whether you own Remove Ads (remove_ads).
- The time the last full-screen ad was shown (to enforce the 3-minute limit).
- How many calculations you have finished (so the first calculation never shows a full-screen ad).

Nothing else is stored. Your calculator inputs are not stored or sent anywhere.

## Ads
MoneyTools shows ads through Google AdMob. Before any ad loads, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for your consent where required. AdMob and Google may collect advertising identifiers and other information as described in Google's privacy policy. Buying Remove Ads removes all ads.

## Purchases
Remove Ads is sold through Google Play Billing as a one-time purchase. Google Play handles payment; we do not see or store your payment details. Use Restore Purchases in Settings to restore it on a new device.

## Support
If you email support@andrewmetry.com, we receive only what you write in that email.
